Pick a few marker colors as your scene objects, set a single light on the color wheel,
then dial its character and strength. The tool works out how each object reads under that
light — highlight, light, halftone, core shadow and reflected light — plus the glow on the
terminator and the contact and cast shadows between objects. Every zone is shown as a real
Enmy marker (or a two-marker mix), so the palette you see is the one you can actually lay down.

Scroll down for the cube and face scenes. Use **Open full screen** for the most room and to
export the palette to PDF.
