## About the event

<div class="lead">
*Flip through sketchbooks. See how ideas actually take shape.*

The second open sketchbook exhibition comes to Batumi for two full days at Loft4.

Across both days, sketchbooks are presented as complete works in their own right – follow how ideas take shape through sketches, experiments, and visual thinking, revealed page by page.

Each sketchbook carries its own style, rhythm, and narrative.

**Come to explore, flip through real sketchbooks, meet the artists, and spend time in a space centered on process and creative exchange.**
</div>


## About the space — Loft4

<div class="lead">
Loft4 is an underground space tucked into a courtyard off Zurab Gorgiladze St – brick walls, graffiti, neon glowing under UV light, a projector running.
</div>

It's not a neutral backdrop. The exhibition lives inside the character of the place: by day the courtyard is bright and welcoming, by night the walls turn into something else entirely.
