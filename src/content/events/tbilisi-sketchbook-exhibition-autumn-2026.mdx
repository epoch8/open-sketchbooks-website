## Open call for artists

We are looking for artists who keep sketchbooks and want to show them at the exhibition. Application deadline is October 22, 2026.

A one-day sketchbook exhibition at ArtHub Tbilisi on October 25, 2026. Sketchbooks are shown as complete artworks: flip through the pages, meet the artists, and see how ideas take shape through sketches.

Artist list and program will be published after the open call closes.
