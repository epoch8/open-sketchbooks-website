Sketchbook 2023-2025,
a retrospective of an artistic search

This sketchbook is meant to be viewed in sequence. It is less a traditional book and more a collection of drawings created at different times, using different techniques, on separate sheets of paper.

Taken together, however, these works form a single narrative – a story of searching for, and reflecting on, the artist’s style and creative direction.
