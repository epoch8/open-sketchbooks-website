To the reckless spender

I’m giving you this Polaroid and some film.
This one uses “600 film” cartridges. They’re a bit pricey, but honestly worth it.

This was my favorite camera – my first “real” Polaroid. Now I’m passing it on to you. I hope it brings you as much joy as it brought me. (You can Google the instructions.)

I hope it comes in handy and that you enjoy it.
