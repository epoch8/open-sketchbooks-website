Sketchbook 2023-2024

This sketchbook can be flipped through in any order.
It doesn’t carry a fixed or explicit meaning – each illustration reflects the artist’s inner state at the moment of drawing.

Like a diary, it doesn’t offer answers, but records thoughts and emotions as visual fragments, tracing an ongoing search for meaning.
