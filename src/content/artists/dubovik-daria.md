### Artist with a background in puppet theatre

I'm drawn to the chance to articulate inner experience and translate it into something tangible. In recent years I've rediscovered the childlike joy of intuitive painting in a naïve style.

### About the sketchbooks

This sketchbook is a diary that came together in one sitting, following an emotional event. In it I reconnected with the vivid images and figures of my inner world — a kind of live broadcast in a naïve style.
