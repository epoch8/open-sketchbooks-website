### Maru — a multidisciplinary artist and maker

My work brings together illustration, design, sculpture, photography, prop making, and mask making into a single visual language. I like turning ideas into tangible objects — a handcrafted sculpture, an expressive mask, a detailed prop, or an original illustration. Inspired by contemporary art, fashion, storytelling, and human emotion, I move between materials and techniques to build immersive experiences.

### About the sketchbooks

*Beetlejuice* is a collection of printed A4 digital sketches inspired by beetles, insects, and other curious creatures. It's a personal exploration of shapes, textures, and character design — a small celebration of the strange beauty of the tiny world around us.
