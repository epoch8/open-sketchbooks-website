### My artistic practice includes illustrating children’s books and collaborating with creative projects, including designer accessories and music brands.

As an independent artist, I explore themes of human intimacy, inner conflict, and transformation through an emotional, sometimes theatrical visual language. I actively work with symbolism, transforming personal experiences and inner processes into illustrations. It is these deep, sometimes painful emotions and transformations that can be seen in my sketchbooks.
