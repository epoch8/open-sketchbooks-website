### I’m an animation artist. 

My drawings are about a love for drawing and self-expression. They can be cute, beautiful, meaningless, silly, or even a bit crazy – they’re about the joy of the process.

As for myself, I’m a traveler living between different countries. At the moment, I’m based in Tbilisi and Riga.

### About the sketchbooks

An A4 sketchbook with black paper – a collection of photos, receipts, candy wrappers, memories, and sketches.

An A5 sketchbook – a collection of original characters, anime drawings, and sketches.

A square sketchbook – drawings made with markers and concepts for paintings that haven’t been created yet.
