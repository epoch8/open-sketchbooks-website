### Drawing since childhood

I always knew my life would be tied to art. I trained as an architect and defended a master's thesis, and the study only deepened my love of visual things. Whenever I leave the house I take a notebook and something to draw with — I believe a drawing of a person carries more about them than a photograph ever could.

### About the sketchbooks

In these sketchbooks I draw everything I see, everything that comes to hand.
